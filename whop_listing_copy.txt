Q1. Do I need to know how to code?
Not at all. BeatScript is a web app — you open it in any browser, fill in a few fields about your beat or brand, and click Generate. No technical knowledge, no installations, no learning curve. If you can type, you can use it.

Q2. Does this work for any genre?
Yes. Whether you make trap, drill, lo-fi, afrobeats, reggaeton, R&B, or anything in between, BeatScript generates copy that fits your sound. Just describe your genre and mood in the input fields and the tools tailor the output to match your style.

Q3. Is there a free trial?
The Starter plan is free forever — not a trial. You get one generation per tool per day at no cost, indefinitely. There's no time limit and no credit card required to get started. The free plan exists so you can see real results before you ever spend a dollar.

Q4. Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, absolutely. Producer Pro is a monthly (or annual) subscription with no lock-in. You can cancel from your Whop account dashboard at any time and you won't be charged again. If you cancel a yearly plan early, you retain access through the end of your billing period.

Q5. What's included in the free plan?
The Starter plan includes access to all five generators — Beat Titles, YouTube SEO Package, Social Media Captions, Producer Bio Writer, and License Pricing Copy — with a limit of one generation per tool per day. It's designed to be genuinely useful, not a teaser.

Q6. How is this different from ChatGPT?
ChatGPT is a general-purpose AI — you have to prompt it, guide it, and edit heavily to get beat-industry-specific output. BeatScript is purpose-built for music producers: the inputs, the outputs, and the underlying prompts are all designed around the beat-selling market. You get better results in fewer clicks, without needing to know how to "talk" to an AI.

Q7. Do you add new generators?
Yes, actively. We have additional tools in development including Email Campaign Copy, BeatStars Profile Optimizer, and a Playlist Pitch Generator. Producer Pro members get early access to every new tool we ship. Follow the product page or join the Pro plan to stay ahead.

Q8. Will this work on mobile?
Yes. BeatScript is fully responsive and works on iPhone, Android, and any tablet browser. Whether you're at the studio or on the go, you can generate and copy content directly from your phone in seconds.
