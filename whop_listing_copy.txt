━━ SECTION 6: FAQ ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q1: Do I need to know how to code?
Not at all. BeatFlow Content Studio runs entirely in your
browser. You fill in a short form — producer name, beat
title, genre, artist style — and click Generate. No accounts,
no setup, no technical knowledge required.

Q2: Does this work for any genre?
Yes. BeatFlow supports Trap, Drill, UK Drill, Melodic Trap,
R&B/Soul, Boom Bap, Afrobeats, Pop, Lofi Hip-Hop, and Jersey
Club — with more genres added based on producer feedback.
Every generator adapts its output to your selected genre and
artist influences so the copy feels specific to your sound.

Q3: Is there a free trial?
There's no trial period — because the free plan is free
forever. You get 1 generation per tool per day at no cost,
no credit card required. Use it as long as you want before
deciding if Pro is worth it.

Q4: Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Producer Pro is month-to-month with no lock-in. Cancel
from your Whop dashboard at any time and you'll retain Pro
access until the end of your current billing period. Annual
subscribers can cancel renewal at any time.

Q5: What's included in the free plan?
The Starter (free) plan includes access to all 5 tools —
Beat Titles, YouTube SEO, Social Captions, Bio Writer, and
License Pricing Copy — with 1 generation per tool per day.
You get the full output quality, just limited to one run
per generator per 24 hours.

Q6: How is this different from ChatGPT?
ChatGPT is a general-purpose tool — you have to engineer
detailed prompts, fix the output, and re-run it multiple
times to get anything usable for beats. BeatFlow is built
specifically for music producers: every generator already
knows what BeatStars needs, how YouTube SEO works for beats,
and what makes license copy convert. You fill in one form
and get production-ready output, every time.

Q7: Do you add new generators?
Yes — new tools are in development, including video script
generators for YouTube Shorts/Reels, cold email templates
for artist outreach, and press kit copy. Producer Pro
subscribers get early access to every new generator as it
launches, included in their existing plan.

Q8: Will this work on mobile?
Yes. BeatFlow Content Studio is fully responsive and works
on iPhone, Android, and any modern mobile browser. Generate
titles, captions, and bios from your phone between sessions
— no desktop required.
